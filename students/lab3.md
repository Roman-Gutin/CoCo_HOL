# Lab 3: Ship the Product

**Duration:** 25 min

> You built the data. You built the brain. Now ship it as a product a business user can talk to.

---

## Prerequisites

- Completed Lab 1 and Lab 2
- Your `ACCOUNT_HEALTH_AGENT` exists

---

## Step 1: Configure for the CRO (5 min)

Open your agent in **Snowsight Intelligence** and configure it as a product:

1. Add **sample questions** — these become clickable prompts the CRO sees when she opens the tool. Think about what questions she'd actually ask.

2. Customize the **response instruction** — tell the agent to name specific people, quote dollar amounts, and suggest deadlines.

3. Add the `data_to_chart` tool so the agent can generate visualizations.

---

## Step 2: Use it as the CRO (8 min)

Experience this as the user, not the builder.

### Click a sample question

Watch the answer stream in. Does it include charts? Account names? Dollar amounts?

### Ask a follow-up

In the same thread, ask the agent to draft an email or dig deeper. Does it remember the context from the prior answer?

### Check renewals

Ask which accounts renew in the next 60 days. Are any of them at risk?

### Explore freely

Ask your own questions. What can you discover?

---

## Step 3: Share it (5 min)

A product isn't a product until someone else can use it.

1. Click **Share** on the agent
2. Grant access to your neighbor's role
3. Have your neighbor open it and ask a question

Does it work? Immediately? Without any setup on their end?

---

## What You Just Did

- Configured an agent as a **product** with sample questions and response instructions
- Experienced **multi-turn conversation** — follow-ups without re-explaining context
- **Shipped it** to a colleague who used it immediately

You built a production intelligence product. As one person. In 90 minutes.
